WARM TEMPERATE (MID-LATITUDE) CLIMATES — GROUP C (context)
Warm temperate (mid-latitude) climates extend from 30°-50° latitude, mainly on the eastern and western margins of continents, generally with warm summers and mild winters. Koeppen groups them into four types: (i) Humid subtropical, dry in winter and hot in summer (Cwa); (ii) Mediterranean (Cs); (iii) Humid subtropical, no dry season and mild winter (Cfa); (iv) Marine west coast climate (Cfb).

MEDITERRANEAN CLIMATE (Cs) — directly corresponds to Goh Cheng Leong's "Warm Temperate Western Margin (Mediterranean) Climate"
As the name suggests, the Mediterranean climate occurs around the Mediterranean Sea, along the west coast of continents in subtropical latitudes between 30°-40° latitude, e.g. Central California, Central Chile, and along the coast in south-eastern and south-western Australia. These areas come under the influence of the subtropical high in summer and the westerly wind in winter. Not much rainfall occurs in any season. Mean average temperature in summer is around 25°C and in winter below 10°C. The annual precipitation ranges between 35-90 cm (350-900 mm) — broadly consistent with, though independently stated from, Goh Cheng Leong's own figures for Rome (838 mm) and Cape Town (635 mm).

(For reference, not used in Ch.19 — reserved for future chapters covering the remaining C-group and D-group climates: Humid Subtropical Cwa occurs poleward of the Tropic of Cancer/Capricorn, mainly in North Indian plains and South China interior plains, similar to Aw climate except winter temperature is warmer; Humid Subtropical Cfa lies on the eastern parts of continents in subtropical latitudes, with unstable air masses causing rainfall throughout the year, found in eastern USA, southern/eastern China, southern Japan, north-eastern Argentina, coastal South Africa, and eastern coast of Australia — matches Goh Cheng Leong's future "Warm Temperate Eastern Margin (China Type) Climate" chapter; Marine West Coast Cfb is located poleward from the Mediterranean climate on the west coast, e.g. north-western Europe, west coast of North America north of California, southern Chile, south-eastern Australia and New Zealand — matches Goh Cheng Leong's future "Cool Temperate Western Margin (British Type) Climate" chapter.)
