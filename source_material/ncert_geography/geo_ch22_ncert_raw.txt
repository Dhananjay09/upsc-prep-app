MARINE WEST COAST CLIMATE (Cfb) — directly corresponds to Goh Cheng Leong's "Cool Temperate Western Margin (British Type) Climate"
Marine west coast climate is located poleward from the Mediterranean climate on the west coast of the continents. The main areas are: north-western Europe, the west coast of North America north of California, southern Chile, south-eastern Australia, and New Zealand — precisely matching Goh Cheng Leong's account of the British Isles, north-west Europe, British Columbia, southern Chile, Tasmania and New Zealand. Due to marine influence, the temperature is moderate, and in winter it is warmer than for its latitude. The mean temperature in summer months ranges from 15°-20°C and in winter 4°-10°C. The annual and daily ranges of temperature are small. Precipitation occurs throughout the year, varying greatly from 50-250 cm.
